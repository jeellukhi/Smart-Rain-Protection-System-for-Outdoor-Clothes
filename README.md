# Smart-Rain-Protrction-System

**Problem Statement**
There is a common issue of clothes getting unexpectedly wet during rain, especially when drying outdoors. In many communities, people rely on outdoor drying to save energy and avoid using electric dryers. However, sudden rain can soak clothes, leading to inconvenience, longer drying times, and even mildew or damage. Our project offers an automated solution that detects rain and promptly moves the drying rack under shelter, ensuring clothes stay dry without constant monitoring.

**Solution Overview**
We developed an automated IoT-based rain protection system designed to protect clothes drying outdoors. This system uses rain sensors to detect rainfall and servo motors to retract clothes under a shelter. When rain is detected, the system promptly signals the servo motors to pull a protective cover over the clothes, ensuring they stay dry. Once the rain stops, the system automatically retracts the cover, allowing natural air drying to resume.
